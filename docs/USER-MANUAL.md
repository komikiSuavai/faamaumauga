# Water Utility Management System - User Manual

## For Customers

### Accessing Your Account

1. **Login**
   - Go to the customer portal
   - Enter your registered email address
   - Enter your password
   - Click "Login"

2. **Dashboard Overview**
   - View your account summary
   - Check recent meter readings
   - See outstanding invoices
   - Track payment history

### Viewing Your Information

#### Account Details
- Click "Account" in the menu
- View your contact information
- Edit phone number (email managed by support)
- Update address if changed

#### Water Consumption
- Click "Consumption" to see:
  - Monthly usage trends
  - Comparison to previous months
  - Cost breakdown
  - Usage recommendations

#### Invoices
- Click "Invoices" to view:
  - All issued invoices
  - Due dates
  - Payment status
  - Download invoice PDF

#### Payments & Receipts
- Click "Payments" to see:
  - Payment history
  - Receipt details
  - Print receipts
  - View payment methods accepted

### Making Payments

1. **View Outstanding Amount**
   - Go to "Invoices"
   - Identify unpaid or partially paid invoices
   - Click on invoice for details

2. **Payment Options**
   - Bank Transfer (preferred)
   - Check payment
   - Cash payment at office
   - Card payment (if available)

3. **Processing Time**
   - Bank transfer: 1-2 business days
   - Cash/Check: Same day or next business day

### Accessing Reports

#### Monthly Statement
- Click "Reports"
- Select "Account Statement"
- Choose month and year
- View or download PDF

#### Consumption Report
- Click "Reports"
- Select "Consumption Report"
- Shows usage trends over selected period

## For Staff Users

### Role-Based Access

#### Admin
- Full system access
- User management
- System configuration
- All reports and data

#### Manager
- Create and manage customers
- Process invoices and receipts
- View office and system reports
- Limited user management

#### Operator
- Record meter readings
- Create invoices
- Process receipts
- View assigned records

#### Viewer
- Read-only access
- View reports
- Cannot modify data

### Common Tasks

#### 1. Register New Customer

1. Click "Customers" → "New Customer"
2. Fill in customer details:
   - First and Last Name
   - Email Address (used for login)
   - Phone Number
   - Complete Address
3. Click "Save"
4. System generates unique custID
5. Send welcome email to customer with temporary password

#### 2. Install Water Meter

1. Click "Meters" → "New Meter"
2. Select customer from dropdown
3. Enter meter details:
   - Model/Type
   - Serial Number (must be unique)
   - Installation Date
   - Location
4. Click "Save"
5. Print meter registration certificate

#### 3. Record Meter Reading

1. Click "Readings" → "New Reading"
2. Select meter from dropdown
3. Enter:
   - Reading Date
   - Current Reading (from meter display)
   - Reading Type (Manual/Automatic)
   - Notes (if any)
4. System calculates consumption
5. Click "Save"

#### 4. Create Invoice

1. Click "Invoices" → "New Invoice"
2. Select:
   - Customer
   - Meter
   - Billing Period dates
3. System auto-fills:
   - Previous reading
   - Current reading
   - Consumption calculation
4. Review rate per unit (edit if necessary)
5. Add taxes/charges if applicable
6. Click "Generate Invoice"
7. System calculates total amount
8. Click "Issue" to send to customer

#### 5. Process Payment Receipt

1. Click "Receipts" → "New Receipt"
2. Select:
   - Invoice number
   - Customer
   - Payment Date
   - Payment Amount
   - Payment Method
3. For bank transfer:
   - Enter reference/check number
4. Click "Verify" to confirm receipt
5. System updates invoice status
6. Print receipt (optional)

### Generating Reports

#### Overdue Invoices Report
1. Click "Reports" → "Overdue Invoices"
2. View all invoices past due date
3. Filter by office (if manager)
4. Export to Excel or PDF

#### Office Performance Report
1. Click "Reports" → "Office Performance" (Manager+)
2. Select office and date range
3. View:
   - Total customers
   - Total receivables
   - Collected amount
   - Payment percentage
   - Average consumption
4. Export for management review

#### Daily Receipt Summary
1. Click "Reports" → "Daily Receipts"
2. Select date
3. View all payments received
4. Reconcile with bank deposits

### Managing Customer Accounts

#### Update Customer Information
1. Click "Customers"
2. Search for customer
3. Click customer name
4. Edit fields as needed
5. Click "Save"

#### Suspend/Reactivate Account
1. Click "Customers"
2. Find customer
3. Click "Actions" → "Change Status"
4. Select new status
5. Enter reason (for audit trail)
6. Confirm

#### View Customer History
1. Click "Customers"
2. Select customer
3. Click "History" tab
4. View all transactions and changes

### User Management (Admin Only)

#### Create New User
1. Click "Settings" → "Users"
2. Click "New User"
3. Enter:
   - Username
   - Email
   - Full Name
   - Office Code
   - Department
   - Role
4. System generates temporary password
5. Send credentials to user via secure email

#### Reset User Password
1. Click "Settings" → "Users"
2. Find user account
3. Click "Actions" → "Reset Password"
4. System generates temporary password
5. User must change on first login

#### Change User Role/Permissions
1. Click "Settings" → "Users"
2. Select user
3. Click "Edit"
4. Change role and office code
5. Click "Save"

## Tips & Best Practices

### For Customers
- Check your consumption monthly
- Pay invoices before due date to avoid penalties
- Update contact information promptly
- Download and save receipts for your records
- Report meter problems immediately

### For Staff
- Always verify customer information before creating records
- Record meter readings on scheduled dates
- Use consistent date formats
- Add notes for unusual readings or adjustments
- Log out when leaving your desk
- Don't share login credentials

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot Login
- Check username/email spelling
- Verify caps lock is off
- Click "Forgot Password" to reset
- Contact support if issues persist

### Missing Information
- Refresh the page (F5)
- Log out and log back in
- Try different browser
- Clear browser cache

### Payment Not Showing
- May take 1-2 days to process
- Check payment reference number
- Contact support with details
